Working forecasts

The future is a set of conditions, not a mood

These are hypotheses for making decisions, not promises about what technology will inevitably become. Each includes the signal that would change my view.

Five forecasts I am testing

1. Verification and recovery become the AI bottleneck

Horizon
Now to three years.
Forecast
As AI moves from drafting answers to taking actions, value will depend less on first-output quality and more on whether people can inspect, interrupt, reverse and recover.
What would change my mind
Independent field data showing sustained autonomous work with low supervision and low-cost recovery across changing conditions.

2. Automation concentrates human work around exceptions

Horizon
One to five years.
Forecast
Routine steps may disappear while the remaining work becomes less frequent, more ambiguous and more consequential. This can raise training and cognitive demands rather than simply reduce labor.
What would change my mind
Evidence that exception rates and time-to-competence fall with automation, without moving the work to unseen reviewers or support staff.

3. Robot autonomy remains a property of the whole deployment

Horizon
Three to ten years.
Forecast
Useful autonomy will be distributed across the machine, operator, environment, operating procedures, maintenance and remote support. A model or robot alone will not explain deployment performance.
What would change my mind
Robots that transfer across materially different sites with little environment preparation, retraining or specialist intervention.

4. Information abundance increases the price of provenance

Horizon
Now to five years.
Forecast
Generating an answer becomes cheap; knowing where it came from, what it omitted and when it stops applying becomes the scarce layer.
What would change my mind
Users and institutions reliably making high-consequence decisions from synthetic answers without demanding traceability, while error and dispute costs remain low.

5. Accessibility becomes augmentation infrastructure

Horizon
Three to ten years.
Forecast
Interfaces that support multiple inputs, adjustable pace, explicit state and recoverable action will matter beyond formal accommodation because AI increases variation in how people receive, verify and act on information.
What would change my mind
Mainstream AI systems sustaining equal task success across human variation without these adaptable interaction properties.

The larger human question

AI may expand what a person can produce while shrinking how much of the process they can understand or contest. The important divide may not be “human versus AI,” but between systems that preserve intent, comprehension, refusal and recovery and systems that trade those away for apparent ease.

That is why I evaluate workload and agency together. Lower effort is useful; lower ability to notice, decide or recover is not the same thing.
