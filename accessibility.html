Accessibility statement

Last updated 2026-08-07 · Self-assessed

Accessibility is part of my work, not a checkbox: I serve as a founding board director of the APAC Authentic Inclusion Association. This site aims for WCAG 2.2 AA and is built to be usable with a keyboard, a screen reader, and your own display preferences.

What is implemented

  • Semantic HTML with landmarks, a skip link, and a logical heading order.
  • All text meets WCAG AA contrast in both light and dark themes; most text exceeds 7:1.
  • Full keyboard operability with visible focus indicators; no keyboard traps.
  • Light and dark themes follow your system preference, with a manual toggle that remembers your choice.
  • prefers-reduced-motion is respected; the site uses no animation beyond optional smooth scrolling.
  • No horizontal scrolling at any viewport width from 320px to 1920px, verified programmatically at seven widths.
  • Text can be resized to 200 percent without loss of content.
  • Traditional Chinese and English versions with correct language tagging.

Known limitations

  • Google Analytics loads a third-party script for basic visit statistics. No advertising features are enabled and no data-sharing options are on.
  • This is a self-assessment supported by automated checks and manual keyboard testing, not yet a third-party audit. A full audit report is planned and will be published here.

Report a barrier

If anything on this site is hard to use with your assistive technology, tell me and I will fix it: chieh.wei.huang@gmail.com
