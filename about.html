About

From how it is built to how it is used.

I am Chieh-Wei Huang (Bertram), based in Japan. My starting point is electronics and production work; my interest is how product choices affect the person using them.

The experience I draw on

Seven years in electrical engineering

At Pegatron, I designed circuit boards, selected components and visited factories to investigate design problems and resolve defects. That work taught me to relate choices on a drawing to actual build conditions and observed failures.

Startup work in Japan

I worked on component sourcing, PCB design and production coordination, including work related to haptic devices and robotics. This is a summary of experience, without using project details whose publication permissions have not been confirmed.

What I am doing now

I continue independent projects and working notes about technology, products and accessibility. I also develop Beacon, a web accessibility testing tool / Skill. This is ongoing work, not a comprehensive compliance certification qualification.

My interests include technology products, consumer electronics, games and accessibility, especially the choices behind how a product is built and used.
