One method across physical and digital systems

  1. Name the decision.
    Specify the next commitment: changing a design, releasing to users, reducing oversight, or increasing investment.
  2. Separate claims from evidence.
    Distinguish measured operation, customer confirmation, vendor reporting, demos, and analogy.
  3. Find the assumptions that matter.
    Investigate tests, manufacturing conditions, human intervention and recovery where they could change the decision.
  4. Set the next gate.
    State what evidence would justify continuing, changing course, or stopping.
