Work with me

Before the next commitment, check what supports it.

A focused technology evidence review for founders, product leads and engineering leads deciding what to release, delegate or fund next.

Email me about your project

Start by email. We agree scope and fee before paid work begins.

Situations we could discuss

  • An agent has made several repairs, but nobody is sure what counts as finished.
  • A prototype works, but you do not know what to test before funding the next batch.
  • You are handing work to a system and need to decide which human checks still matter.

Start with one upcoming decision, not a review of your entire company or codebase. Fit depends on the question, available evidence and expertise required.

What you would receive

A written decision memo, based on the materials and review scope we agree:

  • What the evidence supports: which results apply to the use you intend, and under what conditions.
  • What could change the decision: critical assumptions, unresolved failures and human work the tests may not account for.
  • What to verify next: a proposed test or check, what result would matter, and when the decision should be revisited.

If the available material cannot support a conclusion, the memo will say so. Additional testing or implementation is separately scoped, not silently included.

How I approach the question

First identify the next commitment, then examine what supports it, which assumptions remain untested and what happens if they are wrong. The aim is to identify the few questions that could change the decision and explain what to test next.

My experience includes hardware options and manufacturing trade-offs, robotics projects and AI-assisted tools I build. The case library describes that experience; my first Decision Note shows the current method and its limits.

Start with a short description

What should the system do? What have you observed? Where are you unsure? Include a decision deadline if you have one.

No deck or confidential files needed in the first message. I will reply to establish fit and what material a review would need. If there is a fit, we agree a fixed scope, written deliverable, fee and end date. You do not need to book an ongoing retainer or add me to your team chat.
