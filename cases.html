Selected cases

Judgment under real constraints

These cases are anonymized where the work was confidential. They are organized by the decision problem, not by the component or industry.

AI and machines in physical work

What remains human in a teleoperated robot?

Problem
A retail robot had to work across remote operation, onboard control, store conditions, and field support, not just a controlled demo.
Judgment
Reviewed motor, power, braking, sensing, gripper and compute interfaces as one operating system; traced field failures across electronics, CAN behavior, assembly, supplier and operator conditions.
Result or limit
Supported multi-year deployment and production coordination across Japan, Taiwan and China. The work did not assume autonomy: human intervention and recovery remained part of the system.

Can tactile sensing survive manufacturing?

Problem
A flexible tactile sensor for robot grippers worked as a design concept but carried production risks.
Judgment
Reframed geometry, material and process decisions around what an FPC supplier could repeatedly build and inspect.
Result or limit
The design moved closer to manufacturability. This was production-readiness work, not proof of market demand or long-term field reliability.

When technical readiness is not product readiness

Problem
A force-feedback exoskeleton needed electronics suitable for production while the commercial use case was still uncertain.
Judgment
Improved the electronics and separated production risk from the larger question of whether the market and customer alternatives justified the product.
Result or limit
The electronics improved; commercialization was later shelved. The case became a durable lesson: engineering progress cannot manufacture demand.

Decision and information systems

One choice instead of another feed

Problem
Large game libraries turn leisure into browsing, comparison and decision fatigue.
Judgment
Designed Maida to present one installed game at a time, with explicit ways to play, defer or move on, without a feed to optimize or an infinite list to scan.
Result or limit
A released desktop application in four languages with keyboard, gamepad and screen-reader support. It tests whether a tool can restore a decision; willingness to pay remains unproven.

Evidence boundaries for agent-assisted review

Problem
AI can generate accessibility and discoverability advice faster than it can establish what was actually tested.
Judgment
Built Beacon around reproducible detectors, coverage reporting, weak-evidence states and a strict separation between machine findings and human judgment.
Result or limit
The open tool produces review evidence without calling a partial scan compliance. It supports this practice; accessibility auditing is not the primary offer.

Procurement without pretending the recommendation is certain

Problem
Product comparisons often mix official specifications, search snippets, affiliate content and unspoken preferences.
Judgment
Designed an internal research workflow that requires official sources for price and specification claims, records uncertainty, and keeps the final choice with the user.
Result or limit
The workflow is in active internal use and development. It is evidence of method, not a finished procurement product.

AI-generated geometry needs physical gates

Problem
Code can produce a valid-looking 3D model while missing fit, process, assembly or material reality.
Judgment
Connected code-defined geometry to numeric checks, manufacturable outputs, render inspection and explicit calibration constants.
Result or limit
The pipeline produces inspectable STEP/STL artifacts and records known assumptions. Simulation remains bounded to the tested model; it is not blanket physical validation.

Making changes that survive scale

From engineering change to shipped product

Problem
Notebook and tablet programs had to turn customer changes into buildable, traceable production decisions.
Judgment
Worked across NPI, BOM, ECN, tooling, suppliers and customer-site coordination; later led a four-person team.
Result or limit
Products reached mass production across a seven-year ODM career, including six months at a US customer site. Client and product names remain confidential.

Product intent under power and schedule constraints

Problem
A consumer health device needed its intended experience translated into a PCBA, power architecture and executable schedule.
Judgment
Bound product decisions to electrical limits, development dependencies and production timing rather than treating each as a separate workstream.
Result or limit
Provided the technical and schedule structure for development. This case does not claim medical efficacy or regulatory approval.
