Research discipline

  1. State the decision. Research is scoped around what someone may do differently.
  2. Own the source. A claim must trace to an accessible source, observation or clearly labeled inference.
  3. Read both ways. Each finding gets a supporting interpretation and a credible counter-reading.
  4. Name the reversal signal. A useful conclusion says what new evidence would change it.
  5. Separate absence from failure. “Not measured” is not a pass, and a prototype limit is not proof that the category cannot work.

Accessibility as a stress test

Accessibility matters here because it reveals assumptions about attention, perception, memory, input and recovery. I use it to ask whether an AI-mediated system keeps information and control available under human variation. It is one lens in a broader human-machine evaluation practice, not a compliance sermon or the main offer.
